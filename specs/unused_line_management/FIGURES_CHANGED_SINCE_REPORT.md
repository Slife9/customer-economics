# Figures changed since the report

For updating `Unused_Credit_Line_Management_Project_Report.docx` and `Unused_Credit_Line_Strategy_v2.pptx`.
Stress book (100,000 customers), Base case, default settings after revision E (19 Nov 2026). "Report"
is the figure in the report and slides (reproduced exactly by the system with every revision-E
setting at its old value). Full list: `figures/E_report_figures_stress.md` (generated by
`python audit/unused_line_report_figures.py`).

## Why they moved
1. **Statement-date usage test (1b):** 289 cards that use 30%+ of their limit at statement date leave
   the selection.
2. **Midpoint balance for costs (1b):** the balance outstanding during the month is higher than the
   after-payment balance, so the capital on drawn balances rises and 951 near-break-even cards become
   loss-making and join. Unused credit per card falls.
3. **Actions valued on today's unused line (1b)**, which is 3.8% above the 12-month average.
4. **Pay-in-full cuts out of the approved value (1c):** before, the approved $175k included $14.4k from
   cutting 944 pay-in-full limits, which the report says we don't do; they also made up most of the
   unused credit removed, leavers and capital freed.
5. **The campaign split (2a):** the −$38k for Strategy 2 included the invitation to unused cards
   (−$22k), which now sits in Strategy 3.
6. **The 90-day warning (2b):** Strategy 3's actions now include customers who respond to the warning.
